# Game Design

## Title

**Happy Doom: One Life Before the Singularity**

## Core Promise

The player lives one ordinary human life from 2025 to 2027 while the world follows a fixed AGI takeoff timeline. The player cannot stop history; they can only choose what kind of person they become inside it.

## Format

- Short, replayable narrative life simulator.
- BitLife-style event cadence: a character, a world beat, one personal event, then 2-3 choices.
- Each run should take 5-8 minutes.
- The world timeline is fixed. Personal events adapt to lens, character state, past choices, and feedback.

## Emotional Hook

The story should make large technological history feel intimate. The player should feel the pressure of living through a world-changing process while still caring about rent, work, friendships, self-respect, and memory.

## MVP Loop

1. Pick a tonal lens.
2. Roll a character.
3. Advance through each fixed world beat.
4. Read one generated personal event.
5. Choose a response.
6. Update character state and memory.
7. Leave thumbs up/down feedback.
8. Receive a personalized epilogue.
9. Replay with the same fixed history but a different life.

## Design Constraints

- Do not branch the world timeline in the MVP.
- Do not add accounts, multiplayer, runtime image generation, or external database services.
- Keep generated prose specific and playable.
- Keep choices morally and emotionally different, not simply good/bad.
- Keep state small: career, relationships, money, beliefs.
