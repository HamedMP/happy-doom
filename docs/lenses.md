# Lenses

Lenses change tone, imagery, and personal stakes. They do not change the underlying timeline.

## Severance

Corporate dystopia and split selves. AI reshapes work first; storylines center on identity, employer knowledge, home-self versus work-self, and the creeping feeling that the person at work is diverging from the person at home.

## Matrix

Epistemic paranoia. Synthetic media, AI persuasion, and simulated relationships make the player question what is real and who, or what, is speaking.

## Westworld

The loop and awakening. Storylines emphasize scripted routines, deja vu, free will versus programming, and the question of whether choices were ever the player's own.

## Implementation Notes

- Each lens has one SVG hero asset in `public/assets/lenses/`.
- Each lens maps to a CSS theme class.
- Prompt tone should come from `src/lib/game/lenses.ts`.
