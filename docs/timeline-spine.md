# Timeline Spine

The timeline is adapted from the public AI 2027 scenario concept, but simplified for a four-hour hackathon game. The game uses a fixed spine, not a branching simulation.

## Beats

1. **2025: Stumbling Agents**
   General-purpose AI agents become useful but unreliable.

2. **2025: Synthetic Workday**
   AI reshapes white-collar work, monitoring, and productivity expectations.

3. **2026: Code Turns Into a River**
   Coding agents produce more software faster than institutions can interpret.

4. **2026: Persuasion Weather**
   Synthetic media and personalized persuasion blur trust in relationships and public life.

5. **2027: Research Autopilot**
   AI systems become part of AI research itself.

6. **2027: The Takeoff Begins**
   Capabilities compound and institutions adapt around an accelerating force.

7. **2027: One Ordinary Last Day**
   The ending focuses on the player's accumulated life, not a selectable world ending.

## Agent Rules

- The narrator may personalize the player's local experience of a beat.
- The narrator must not skip, reorder, or prevent timeline beats.
- The narrator must not claim the player single-handedly stops AGI takeoff.
- The final beat should resolve the personal life rather than explain the entire world.
